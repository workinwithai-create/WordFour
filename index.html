🎤 Mic / Guitar

Not connected. WordFour only uses your mic or interface after you press Connect.

Record loops whatever the Loop box says (one section or the whole song). Every pass becomes a new take for the instrument you played. Keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J K, Z / X octave, Space play/stop. A MIDI keyboard works with velocity and the sustain pedal. The voice sample sings "ooh"; your words are on screen, in the MIDI lyric events and on the song sheet.
