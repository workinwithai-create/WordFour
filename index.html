WordFour

You've got the words but no melody, and no music theory to find one. Type your lyric, set your BPM, key and 4 chords, and WordFour gives you two ways in. Sketch builds a starting melody from the words themselves: stressed syllables land on beats and chord tones, and the last word comes home to the root. Play lets you hum it out on any key: every press sings your next syllable at the pitch you play, on a real sampled voice over a real band. Every loop pass is a new take. Export a WAV, a MIDI file with your lyric attached to every note, and a lyric sheet. Nothing is uploaded, no AI sounds.

Your words

The sketch is a deterministic starting point from the stress of your words, not a finished song. Press B to hear it, then play your own.

Sing it on the keys

One bar of count-in, then play. Recording loops until you stop it, every 4-bar pass becomes its own take, and each pass starts again from your first word. Keys: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P ; ' play C up to F, Z / X change octave. A MIDI keyboard works too, with velocity and the sustain pedal. Honest note: the sample sings "ooh"; your words show here, on the grid, in the MIDI lyric events and on the lyric sheet.
