# WordFour

**Paste a whole song. Get an arrangement. Sing it on the keys.** WordFour is for writers with a lyric and no melody or band. Paste the full lyric and WordFour finds the sections, builds a song around them, explains every arrangement move, and lets you change all of it.

- **Sections from your lyric.** A blank line starts a new block. `[Verse]`, `[Chorus]`, `[Bridge]` style labels work, and a bare `[Chorus]` repeats the last chorus. Without labels, blocks that repeat most become the chorus. Blocks just before a chorus become pre-choruses, and the rest become verses, a bridge and an outro. Each section has its own bars (2 per lyric line, 4 to 16), chords (1 to 8, one per bar) and feel.
- **Suggested moves, each with a reason.** These come from the WorkinWithAI desks: half-time bridge (HalfFour), double-time second chorus (DoubleFour: snare on the "and"s, 16th hats, 8th bass, strings at the old rate), fills into choruses (RollFour), snare builds out of pre-choruses (LiftTwo), a stop bar before the last chorus (HoleFour), a drop-out at the top of verse 2 (AirCut), sparse or full band, who plays where, and a +2 last-chorus lift with a crash (ModEight, UpStep). Click a section on the timeline to see why each move is there.
- **You decide.** ✎ Edit section changes name, type, bars, chords, feel, band, last-bar move, key lift, drop-out, who plays and the lyric lines. You can move, duplicate, add and remove sections. 🔒 locks any field so that ↻ Re-suggest keeps it. ↶ Undo (or Ctrl+Z) goes back up to 60 steps. Everything is saved in your browser.
- **One tempo.** Half-time and double-time are feels on the same grid. The BPM never changes, and the MIDI has a single tempo event.
- **Play and sing.** The default instrument is **Sing lyrics**: every key sings the next syllable of the section under the playhead, at the pitch you play, on real FluidR3 Voice Oohs (it sings "ooh"; your words are shown karaoke-style and travel in the MIDI and sheet). You can also play piano, electric piano, nylon guitar or clean electric guitar through the amp, bass through the bass amp, strings, or a drum kit (kick, snare, hat, crash). Play on 13 on-screen keys, the computer keys `A W S E D F T G Y H U J K` (Z/X change octave, Space plays and stops) or a MIDI keyboard with velocity and sustain. Notes never start the transport. There are "Your playing", "Band" and "Master" faders into a limiter.
- **Takes.** ● Record loops the whole song or one section (Loop box). Every pass becomes a new take for each instrument you played. Sections without a sung take use a sketch melody generated from the stress of your words. ✨ Sketch saves one as a take.
- **Vocal chains and amps.** The vocal chains are Clean Pop, Warm R&B, Rock Grit, Lo-fi and Radio. Each has a high-pass, subtractive EQ, compressor, split-band de-esser, presence/air, saturation, a synthetic plate or room reverb send and a tempo delay. The amps are Clean Jazz, Edge of Breakup, Classic Crunch, High Gain and Bass amp. Each has trim, gate, 4x-oversampled drive, a tone stack, a synthetic cab, chorus, delay and reverb. Every stage can be switched off and has big knobs. The names are plain and these are not models of any branded gear. Takes are stored **dry** and the chain is a setting on each take, so you can change it after recording. Live and export use the same code.
- **Exports** (`wordfour-<recipe>-<bpm>bpm-<key>`):
  - WAV: the full song, 48 kHz 24-bit, exact length, peak at −1.1 dBFS.
  - MIDI: one tempo, a marker per section, one track per band chair and per take, with your lyric as lyric events.
  - Song sheet: sections, moves, chords per bar, lyric and the notes of each syllable.
  - Stems .zip: band chairs plus each take processed, with an optional dry copy. Every stem peaks at or below −1 dBFS.
- **Engine rules.** An audio-clock lookahead scheduler runs with LOOKAHEAD_MS 25 and SCHEDULE_AHEAD 0.12. Live changes land within a bar, and loop changes land on the next bar line. A/B are matched in loudness. Hiding the tab stops playback. Samples are pinned: FluidR3_GM (CC BY 3.0) at `gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts@044fab8e1456bfafc5776e86dfd6bb8697149aef`, and Virtuosity Drums (CC0) at `sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums@9f04cf9a734527edfbb0a4eee1f674e45bbf71bc`. Every semitone used is sampled. If a sample is missing, the app names it and disables play and export.
- **Price: $19 one-time** (Lemon Squeezy license key). The free demo plays, records and exports MIDI and the sheet for the whole song. WAV and stems cover the first 2 sections until you unlock. Checkout is configured in `LEMON` in `app.js`; those values are placeholders until the product exists.

Static HTML, CSS and JS (`fx.js`, `song.js`, `app.js`) with no framework, auth or database. Run it with `python3 -m http.server`. Tests are in `qa/` (Playwright, Python): `s9.py` covers the 7 engine checks, and `v2.py` covers sections, moves, editing, feels, exports, instruments and FX. Code is MIT; the samples keep their own licenses.

## Mic and guitar input (aligned audio takes)

- In **More → Mic / guitar in**, choose what you plug in: **Mic → vocal chain**, **Guitar → amp** or **Bass → bass amp**. Pick the device and press **Connect input**. The browser only asks for the mic after you press it. Echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto gain are all off, so you get a clean DI or mic signal.
- You get a level meter, a trim control and a **Hear yourself** monitor. The monitor runs through the live chain, so use headphones with it.
- **Calibrate** plays 8 clicks and listens for them on the input. It then stores the round-trip offset as the **Nudge**. **Auto latency** adds the browser's reported output and input latency.
- When an input is connected, **● Record** also records it for every pass of the loop (one section or the full song). Each pass becomes an audio take, cut to the exact pass length, shifted by the offset and saved **dry** in your browser (IndexedDB, nothing is uploaded). The vocal chain or amp is applied on playback, so you can change presets after recording. Live playback and offline export use the same chain.
- Audio takes play in the live mix, the WAV export and the stems (processed + dry). A mic take replaces the sketch melody for the sections it covers. Delete a take or clear all takes to remove its audio.
- Test: `python3 qa/ain.py URL out.json` sends clicks at grid times through a MediaStream and checks the take length, steadiness, on-grid error after calibration and stem alignment.
