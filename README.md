# WordFour

**You've got the words, now play the melody.** Plenty of writers have a notebook full of lyrics and no melody, and no music theory to find one. In WordFour you type your lyric, set your BPM, key and 4 chords, and get two ways in:

- **Sketch from the words.** WordFour splits your lyric into syllables and works out which ones are stressed (`wait-ing`: hyphens override the auto split). It then lays out a deterministic starting melody: one phrase per line, stressed syllables on the beat, strong and on-beat syllables on the bar's chord tones, everything else in key, and the last word resolving to the chord root. You can choose the shape (Arch, Rise, Fall or Wave) and keep it as a take with "Use sketch as a take". It's a starting point, not a finished song.
- **Play it, one syllable per key.** In Words mode, *every key you press sings your next syllable* at the pitch you play. You can use the 18 on-screen keys, the computer keys `A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P ; '` (Z/X change octave) or any MIDI keyboard, with velocity and the sustain pedal (CC64). It plays one note at a time, like a singer. The current word lights up in a karaoke strip, and the next syllable is always shown.
- **Honest voice.** The melody is played on real FluidR3 "Voice Oohs" samples (Flute or Piano are also available). It sings "ooh", not your words. Your words are attached to every note on the grid, in the MIDI file as lyric meta events (FF 05), and on the lyric sheet.
- **Your loop first.** BPM is 60 to 180. There are 12 keys, and the 4-chord entry takes chords like `Am F C G` (m, 7, m7, maj7, dim, sus2, sus4). These settings persist in localStorage and drive playback and every export. The presets (Porch Light 92, Slow Ballad 68, Night Drive 112) are only demos.
- **Looping recorder.** Press ● Record for a one-bar count-in. The loop then runs until you stop it. **Each 4-bar pass becomes its own take and starts again from your first word.** Each take can be set to Loop or Silent, muted, renamed, edited (tap a note to delete it) or deleted. By default the newest take loops and older ones go silent; turn on "Layer" to stack them. Up to 16 takes persist.
- **Recipes.** Choose 16ths in key (the default), 8ths in key, 16ths landing on chords (on-beat notes snap to chord tones), or Your feel (no quantize, no snap). One pure function feeds live playback, the WAV, the MIDI and the sheet.
- **A/B at matched loudness.** A (Thin) is the band only. B (Need) is the band plus your melody, with makeup gain measured offline so the louder one doesn't win by volume alone.
- **Live play-along.** In Words mode, or plain note modes for voice, flute, piano, bass and pad, notes never start the transport. MIDI Start and Stop only run it if you opt in. Web MIDI in and out device selects persist. MIDI out sends bass on ch1, piano on ch2, pad on ch3, melody on ch4 and kit on ch10.
- **Audio output select.** Uses `AudioContext.setSinkId` where the browser supports it. Otherwise the app says it is using the system output. **There is no audio input; WordFour never uses the mic.**
- **Chairs (real samples, pinned).** Voice oohs, flute, acoustic grand piano, finger bass and string ensemble come from FluidR3_GM (Frank Wen, CC BY 3.0): `gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts@044fab8e1456bfafc5776e86dfd6bb8697149aef`. The backing kit (kick, snare and closed hi-hat) is Virtuosity Drums, CC0: `sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums@9f04cf9a734527edfbb0a4eee1f674e45bbf71bc`. Every semitone used is sampled, so nothing is pitch-shifted, and the engine refuses any shift beyond ±4 semitones. If a sample fails to load, the app names the missing instrument and disables play, record and export.
- **Export.** `wordfour-<recipe>-<bpm>bpm-<key>.wav` is a 48 kHz, 24-bit stereo WAV. The first downbeat is at sample 0 and the length is exact. The ring-out tail is folded back into the start, compressor latency is removed, and the peak sits at -1.1 dBFS. The matching `.mid` is format 1 with tempo and 4/4, one track per chair, and **one melody track per take with your lyric on every note**. `…-lyrics.txt` lists each bar's chord with every syllable, its note and its bar.beat.16th position. "Export takes" saves a .zip of per-take melody-only WAV stems.
- **Timing.** An AudioContext lookahead scheduler runs with LOOKAHEAD_MS 25 and SCHEDULE_AHEAD 0.12. Live changes apply on the next step. Hiding the tab stops playback cleanly.
- **Price: $19 one-time** (Lemon Squeezy license key). The free demo has everything at 4 bars: lyrics, sketches, recording, all takes, MIDI and lyric-sheet export, MIDI I/O, WAV and stems. A license unlocks 8- and 16-bar WAV and stems. Checkout is configured in `LEMON` in `app.js` (`checkoutUrl`, `storeId`, `productId`). These are **placeholders until the product exists**. Until then, no key unlocks anything and the Buy button reads "Checkout opening soon". A key unlocks only if its store and product IDs match, and it is re-validated on load.

Static HTML, CSS and JS with no framework, auth or database. Run it locally with `python3 -m http.server` and open http://localhost:8000. Tests are in `qa/` (Playwright, Python). Code is MIT; the samples keep their own licenses.
